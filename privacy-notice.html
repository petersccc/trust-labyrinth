Welche Daten erhoben werden

Wenn Sie das Vertrauenslabyrinth durchlaufen, ohne Ihre E-Mail-Adresse zu hinterlassen, werden Ihre Antworten mit keinen Informationen verknüpft, die Sie identifizieren. Ausschließlich, um Ihnen die gewünschten persönlichen nächsten Schritte zu senden, und, falls Sie anschließend ein Kennenlerngespräch buchen, um dieses Gespräch vorzubereiten und nachzubereiten.

Für den Versand Ihrer persönlichen nächsten Schritte: Ihre ausdrückliche Einwilligung, die Sie über das Kontrollkästchen bei der Eingabe Ihrer E-Mail-Adresse erteilen (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a DSGVO). Sie können diese Einwilligung jederzeit widerrufen, siehe „Ihre Rechte“. Der Widerruf berührt nicht die Rechtmäßigkeit dessen, was Ihnen bis dahin bereits zugesandt wurde.

Für die Vorbereitung und Nachbereitung eines von Ihnen gebuchten Kennenlerngesprächs: Diese Verarbeitung ist für vorvertragliche Maßnahmen erforderlich, die auf Ihre Anfrage erfolgen (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO), und ist nicht Teil der Einwilligung über das Kontrollkästchen.

What this tool collects

If you go through the Trust Labyrinth without leaving your email, your answers are not linked to any identifying information.   • Your company name
  • Your email address
  • Your answers in each room of the labyrinth
  • Your answer to the question "What would you try first in your company?", if you choose to write one
  • The date you submitted them

The question "What would you try first in your company?" is optional. Please do not enter names or other personal details about colleagues there.

Why we process this data

Only to send you the personal next steps you asked for, and, if you go on to book a discovery call, to prepare for and follow up on that conversation.

Legal basis

For sending your personal next steps: your explicit consent, given through the checkbox shown at the point you submit your email (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw this consent at any time, see Your rights below. Withdrawing it does not affect anything already sent to you before that point.

For preparing and following up on a discovery call you choose to book: this is necessary to take steps you have asked for ahead of a potential working relationship (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and it is not covered by the checkbox itself.

Whether AI processes your answers

No AI system processes your individual answers when you use this tool. Your result on the screen is calculated from the cards you choose, and the texts you see were written and reviewed by a person in advance. Your personal next steps are written by Katarina Peters herself.

Who else receives this data

  • Cloudflare, which hosts this website
  • ClickUp, where your email, answers and optional text are stored if you submit them
  • Calendly, if you choose to book a discovery call through the link provided

Each of these acts as a data processor on our behalf, under its own data processing agreement.

Whether data leaves the EU or EEA

Yes. Cloudflare, ClickUp and Calendly are all US based and process data there. Each relies on the Standard Contractual Clauses, the transfer safeguard recognized under GDPR, and Cloudflare and Calendly are also certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework as an additional basis. These terms are already built into each provider's standard agreement, not negotiated separately.

How long we keep your data

If you leave your email and answers but never book a call and don't respond to any follow up, this record is deleted after 12 months. If you do respond, or become a client, your information is kept as part of normal business records instead, governed separately from this notice.
